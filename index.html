ABOUT ME

I'm Intijar Ansari, a dedicated Production Merchant currently associated with Shivank Udyog Ltd., Gurugram, and originally based in New Delhi, India. Since October 2021, I've specialized in end-to-end garment production — from costing and tech pack review to fabric & trims sourcing, vendor coordination, and on-time delivery for global fashion brands across Men's, Women's and Kids wear categories.

Production & Sourcing

Reviewing tech packs, Fit & PP samples, and sourcing fabric & trims from India, China and Gurugram.

Costing & Vendor Coordination

Preparing costing, negotiating trims, and running PPMs with Quality and PPC teams.

T&A & ERP Management

Tracking daily T&A on PLM & SRM portals and maintaining accurate data in ERP (TEXMIA).

"I take pride in turning a tech pack into a perfectly executed garment — bridging the factory floor and global buyers with precision and care."
